# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People and trusted family caregivers running a private local OpenCare installation. They use the
Workspace to organize one Person's recorded health context, prepare for visits, and manage explicit
access without exposing other People on the installation.

## Product Purpose

OpenCare is a local-first toolkit for evidence-grounded health AI workflows. The live product keeps
medical records behind actor sessions and explicit Person assignments. It is not a diagnostic,
treatment, medication-selection, or dosage-recommendation system.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and private. Copy should state what the system does, what it cannot do, and which
identity or Person context is active.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a public SaaS account portal, an engagement-driven consumer health app, or a generic
AI dashboard. Avoid decorative security language, hidden authorization behavior, and visual changes
that make the live Workspace look like the synthetic demo.

## Design Principles

- Keep Person context and access consequences visible.
- Make high-risk grants and exports explicit before submission.
- Reveal only records the current Actor can access; never use hidden totals as empty-state copy.
- Preserve the existing Workspace vocabulary and keep demo/reviewer routes visibly separate.
- Prefer familiar forms and plain recovery guidance over novel controls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, associated form labels, keyboard-visible focus, live status regions, readable
contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion behavior. Do not rely on color alone for permission,
error, or success states.
